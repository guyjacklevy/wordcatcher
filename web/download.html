Word Catcher for Mac

Select any English word or sentence on your Mac and press one shortcut: get the Hebrew, the meaning in your sentence, and better English for what you write. Every word is saved for a quick daily review on your phone.

You need a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or newer), macOS 14 or later, and an invite code.

Download for Mac

Set it up (2 minutes)

  1. Open the downloaded file. It unzips to WordCatcher. Drag it into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. macOS says it can't check the developer: click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to WordCatcher, and confirm. You only do this once.
  3. When asked, allow Accessibility (System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility → turn on WordCatcher). This lets it read the text you select.
  4. In the window that opens, click Have an invite? Create an account and enter your email, a password and your invite code.
  5. Select a word anywhere and press ⌃ ⌥ T (Control + Option + T).
  6. On your iPhone, open wordcatcher-web.vercel.app in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen, and sign in with the same email and password.

What it does

  • A word you don't know: the Hebrew and what it means in your sentence. Saved automatically.
  • A whole sentence: the sentence in Hebrew, and tap the words you want to learn.
  • Writing, and only know the word in Hebrew? Type it in Hebrew inside your sentence: you get the English that fits, and Use it puts it in.
  • English you wrote: a smoother, correct version, with what changed and why.
  • On your phone: a few minutes a day to review your own words, in your own sentences.
